% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
% Core
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\desktop{nucleu}{brandNavy}{Core}{Core: employees \& organisation}{%
  The personnel register of the demo company, with roles, filters and Excel
  import of employees (column mapping and a per-row validation report). The
  core also holds departments, the org chart, work locations, personnel files
  and an append-only audit log; one user can belong to several companies and
  switch between them without signing out.}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
% HR
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\desktop{attendance}{brandBlue}{HR}{Attendance (time tracking)}{%
  Monthly timesheet for August 2026. Attendance covers a monthly timesheet and
  weekly plan, overtime and night hours, approval by department or week, and
  month locking and archiving; employees clock in from their phone (see the
  last two pages).}

\desktop{leave}{brandBlue}{HR}{Leave}{%
  Team leave calendar for one month. Requests go through a configurable
  approval chain, balances are computed automatically with public holidays
  excluded, and 11 leave types are seeded, each with a legal-basis note that is
  being checked together with the payroll values.}

\desktop{reges}{brandBlue}{HR}{REGES-Online filing}{%
  REGES-Online is the Labour Inspectorate's (ITM) mandatory electronic employee
  register: every hire, salary change, suspension and termination must be filed
  there. This is the connection screen of the demo company, which is
  deliberately not connected. Each employer enters its own API credentials
  from the REGES portal and chooses the test or production environment; they
  are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Events are composed from the employee
  record into a queue with legal deadlines, and messages carrying an
  employee's personal ID number are sent by a person with transmit rights.
  Filing through the API has worked in production with a real company since
  17 September 2026.}

\desktop{onboarding}{brandBlue}{HR}{Onboarding / offboarding}{%
  Onboarding journeys in progress. Each journey follows a template of
  reorderable steps (a tick, a document or a signature) and produces a
  printable proof of completion.}

\desktop{courses}{brandBlue}{HR}{Courses}{%
  The company's course library, with PDFs and videos consumed inside the
  application. Completion is proven per item by a tick, the measured percentage
  watched or a signed declaration, and recertification can be scheduled.}

\desktop{evaluations}{brandBlue}{HR}{Performance reviews}{%
  Annual reviews, built from review templates with criteria defined by the
  company, with the history kept per employee.}

\desktop{kpi}{brandBlue}{HR}{KPIs}{%
  One month of KPIs (August 2026). Monthly targets per employee or team come
  from reusable indicator sets; closed months cannot be changed, and each
  employee sees their own KPIs in the employee portal.}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
% Finance
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\desktop{payroll}{brandTeal}{Finance}{Payroll}{%
  An approved payroll period with its deliverables: SEPA bank file, accounting
  note (CSV), payroll register (PDF) and the D112 monthly contributions return
  (XML, generated but not filed with the tax authority). Payroll is built end
  to end, with every rate held in dated, versioned settings; its legal values
  are undergoing accountant validation.}

\desktop{per_diem}{brandTeal}{Finance}{Travel \& per diem}{%
  Business trips with their estimated per diem. Travel orders support
  multi-country legs, 24-hour windows counted from departure, country rates and
  the split at the tax-free ceiling, with a printable expense report.}

\desktop{rapoarte}{brandTeal}{Finance}{Reports}{%
  Annual aggregated report built from closed payroll periods. Company-wide
  aggregates (income, leave, meal vouchers) are computed in the database and
  export to Excel.}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
% Operations
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\desktop{fleet}{brandSlate}{Operations}{Fleet}{%
  Company vehicles, each with the first of its documents to expire
  (inspection, insurance, road tax). Trip sheets record odometer and fuel; the
  database blocks an odometer going backwards and flags large jumps.}

\desktop{maintenance}{brandSlate}{Operations}{Maintenance}{%
  The maintenance dashboard. Servicing is planned by days and by counter
  (hours, km, cycles), with fault reports, parts and labour cost, and ISCIR
  authorisations (the state inspectorate for boilers, pressure vessels and
  lifting equipment).}

\desktop{inventory}{brandSlate}{Operations}{Inventory}{%
  Inventory items and who holds them. Each allocation has a handover date, the
  employee confirms receipt, and the database prevents one item from being
  allocated to two people at once.}

\desktop{ssm}{brandSlate}{Operations}{Health \& safety (SSM/PSI)}{%
  The occupational health \& safety briefing matrix (employee × briefing
  type), with a due-date traffic light that tells ``never done'' apart from
  ``expired''. The module also covers occupational medicine, accidents with a
  reporting countdown, fire extinguishers, PPE and nominal authorisations.}

\desktop{ticketing}{brandSlate}{Operations}{IT ticketing}{%
  The team's ticket queue, triaged across software, hardware, faults linked to
  an inventory item and in-app bug reports. Employees see their own tickets in
  the employee portal.}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
% Communication
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\desktop{announcements}{brandAmber}{Communication}{Announcements}{%
  Published internal announcements, with the pinned one on top. Read
  confirmations are counted against the number of active employees, and every
  active member receives an in-app notification.}
