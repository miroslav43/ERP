\sectiune{4. If the round is a convertible loan instead}
{\small\RaggedRight\color{brandNavy}
A CLA of the same €250,000 with the priced pre-money (€1,250,000) as its cap, no
interest and the same 10\,\% pool gives the founders the \textbf{same 58.67\,\%} after
the illustrative seed: the cap price is below the discounted seed price, so the
illustrative 20\,\% discount plays no part. A CLA is not founder-friendlier:
interest or a binding discount would dilute the founders further. The ``CLA
alternative'' sheet of cap-table.xlsx shows the conversion step by step, with
the same pool units as the priced round. Terms follow Vest
Ventures' templates [TO CONFIRM]. A €220,000 round (the Vest Ventures ticket plus
the minimum 10\,\% private match) would mean 15.0\,\% dilution at the same
pre-money.\par}

\nota{1}\,\textbf{Why the company changes form at this round.} In an SRL, any amendment of the articles, including each capital increase for a new investor, needs the vote of \emph{all} associates unless the articles say otherwise (art.\ 192). We plan to convert to a joint-stock company (SA) or a holding at this round: majority votes in an extraordinary general meeting, shares that transfer through the register, and an option plan that works naturally; an SA needs a minimum share capital of RON 90,000 (art.\ 10) and auditors or censors. If the company stays an SRL at signing, the same act allows majority decisions (for example 3/4 of capital) and drag-along. Source: Legea 31/1990 privind societățile (Companies Law), on legislatie.just.ro. Legal and tax steps [TO CONFIRM] with a lawyer and an accountant.\par\vspace{2pt}
\nota{2}\,\textbf{Illustrative seed:} €1,000,000 at €4,000,000 pre-money (€5,000,000 post-money), no top-up of the option pool; not a plan and not a commitment. Percentages are rounded to two decimals, so a column may not add up to exactly 100.00\,\%. Sources: cap-table.xlsx (sheets ``Cap table'' and ``CLA alternative'') and financial-model.xlsx.\par
