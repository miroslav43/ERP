Every financial figure comes from the financial model; market figures carry their own sources, listed in the appendix of the business plan. Anything dated after 6 October 2026 is a projection. We have zero paying customers: the product has been live since early September 2026 and an accountant pilot opened on 6 October 2026.

REGES-Online is the electronic employee register that every Romanian employer files with the Labour Inspectorate (ITM). Currency is EUR; RON is converted at 5 RON = 1 EUR.
