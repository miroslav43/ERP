# ADMINISTRATIVO — Data room

Data room for ADMINISTRATIVO's pre-seed application (prepared 6 Oct 2026).
ADMINISTRATIVO (administrativo.ro) is HR and payroll software for Romanian SMEs.
Staff clock in from their phone, leave and payroll calculate themselves, and
filings go to REGES-Online, the electronic employee register of the Romanian
Labour Inspectorate (ITM).

How to read this room:

- Every financial figure comes from the financial model
  (`financial-model.xlsx`); market figures carry their own sources, listed in
  the appendix of the business plan. Anything dated after 6 Oct 2026 is a
  **projection**.
- We have **zero paying customers** today. The product has been live in
  production since early September 2026. An accountant pilot opened on
  6 October 2026.
- Currency is EUR. RON is converted at 5 RON = 1 EUR.
- Links are relative to this folder. A browsable version of this index is in
  [`index.html`](index.html).

Status legend: **available** = the file is in this room · **online** = public
page on administrativo.ro · **to be uploaded** = will be added before due
diligence.
